Essential Operators

OperatorPurposeExample
site:Limit search to a domain or TLDsite:github.com
inurl:Match keywords in the URL pathinurl:admin.php
intitle:Match keywords in page <title>intitle:"index of /"
intext:Match keywords in visible page textintext:"secret key"
filetype:Search specific file extensionsfiletype:pdf OR filetype:env
ext:Synonym for filetypeext:sql

SyntaxFunctionExample
